motion-studio

motion-studio is a personal video tool. It makes short animated videos and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

It is not a public service. Only its owner signs in to it, and it acts only on the channel that person chooses when signing in.

What it does with YouTube: uploads videos (private by default), sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and privacy, and reads the signed-in channel's name and ID to check it is uploading to the right channel.
