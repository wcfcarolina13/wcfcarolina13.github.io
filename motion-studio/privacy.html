Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

motion-studio is a personal tool used by its owner to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services.

What it accesses

When you sign in with Google, motion-studio asks for permission to upload and manage videos on the YouTube channel you choose, and to read that channel's name and ID. It does not access your email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

Where your data goes

The sign-in token is stored only on the computer that signed in. motion-studio has no server. It sends data only to Google's YouTube API, and only to upload or update videos you tell it to. It does not sell, share or analyse your data, and it shows no ads.

How to remove access

You can revoke motion-studio's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on your computer also removes it.

Google and YouTube

Use of YouTube through motion-studio is also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
